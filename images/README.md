# Guide screenshots

These images accompany the [user guide](../user-guide.md) and
[quick start](../quick-start.md). They are unmodified app captures or SwiftUI screen
and component snapshots. They contain demonstration/test data, not private household
records. Booking codes and travelers shown are invented; venue and travel details are
illustrative rather than verified advice.

The full-phone captures came from the app's fictional London teaser scenario.
The component captures came from its visual regression references. The recommendation
example intentionally shows an unloaded image placeholder. Some examples were captured
on different dates or iOS versions; check labels and behavior before replacing them.
